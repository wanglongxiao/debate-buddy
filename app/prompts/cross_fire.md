Create 6 to 10 short questions. Every question must:
- be easy for a Grade 7 student to ask aloud;
- have one clear purpose;
- include a follow-up for both a yes answer and a no answer;
- identify the best speaker role for using it;
- test an assumption, impact, example, or contradiction.

Do not make a speech inside a question. Do not ask several questions at once. Include what the
student should keep emphasizing and what traps or unhelpful topics to avoid.
