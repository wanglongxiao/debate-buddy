# Curated WSD training notes

Based on the user's HMDC glossary and speaker training material, cross-checked
against the WSDC Board Speaker Training adapted for Serbia 2024:
https://www.wsdcdebating.org/_files/ugd/669183_97fad98c6ab6452e9dd587a18ca9f953.pdf
HMDC resources: https://hmdcforensics.com/wsd-resources/
These notes are coaching context, not evidence about any motion. Illustrative
quotes and pet statistics in training examples must never become sourced facts.

- Classify the motion before assigning burdens. Policy evaluates a feasible
  change against the baseline; actor-policy evaluates a particular actor's
  choice under its actual incentives, knowledge and constraints. Value needs a
  fair evaluative criterion; comparison needs equivalent scope. Regret compares
  the actual development with a plausible world without it, including benefits
  lost. Prediction concerns likelihood rather than desirability.
- Definitions must preserve fair ground. First opposition rebuts the case and
  challenges definitions only when unfair, explaining a fair alternative.
  Opposition may defeat a necessary proposition burden without proving the
  exact inverse of every proposition claim.
- A substantive has a tagline, claim, evidence, causal warrants, impact and
  comparison. Explain why actors behave as claimed and why the resulting
  impact answers the motion. Evidence alone does not establish a mechanism.
- Direct refutation targets a specific premise, causal link or impact.
  Mitigation reduces scale or likelihood; concession plus weighing accepts a
  limited point but explains why another impact matters more. A missing source
  is an uncertainty, not proof of falsity. Address the strongest opposing case.
- Clash comparison explains both sides and chooses relevant criteria such as
  probability, scale, time, distribution, principle and reversibility. Explain
  why a criterion takes priority here and why the decisive burden is met even
  if a smaller clash is lost. Do not declare a winner without reasoning.
- First lays foundations; second rebuts and extends; third prioritizes rebuttal
  and clash comparison; reply crystallizes the debate. Our preparation strategy
  reserves a distinct third core argument for second and introduces no new
  independent case in third or new facts in reply. This is a conservative
  coaching strategy: WSDC permits third-speaker material flagged in first and
  derivative reply weighing/framing/examples from the established case.
- WSD POIs occur between the first and last minute of substantive speeches,
  last at most 15 seconds, and are not offered in reply. Timing advice must be
  conditional when no clock is available. Accepting about two is guidance,
  not an automatic-loss rule.
- Use numbered signposts, a coherent team caseline and clear transitions.
  Style, content and strategy all matter; speaking style cannot replace
  warranted reasoning and engagement. A preparation draft predicts possible
  objections; a live record attributes only what was actually heard.

The shared application prompt in app/services/debate_framework.py applies these
notes to Pack generation/refinement, role drafts, speech export and Live advice.
